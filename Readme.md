# Rabbit Hole AI

**Rabbit Hole AI** is a privacy-first research tool that replaces YouTube’s context-sensitive algorithm with AI-driven search results, helping you stay focused and in control.

## Features

### AI-Powered Search Context
- Uses AI to generate search results based on your prior searches within a thread.
- Keeps your research on-topic and free from YouTube's distraction-based recommendations.

### Thread-Based Organization
- Each topic is stored in a thread, allowing for organized, structured research.
- You can bookmark videos in threads and trim down threads to refine how the AI generates future results.

### In-App Video Viewing
- Watch YouTube videos directly within the app.
- Choose between watching via YouTube or a privacy-focused proxy that hides your data from YouTube.
- Uses the official youtube api to get search results, so you still harness the youtube algorithm.
### Privacy and Data Ownership
- All history is saved locally on your device.
- The Rabbit Hole server acts only as a proxy—YouTube never sees your search queries.
- Your data is protected with a hashed passkey stored securely on disk.

### Take Control of Your Attention
- Rabbit Hole encourages you to actively manage and curate your content experience.
- Instead of being influenced by algorithms, you become the driver of your own discovery.
- Dont get distracted by the youtube alrogithm, go down your own rabbit hole.
